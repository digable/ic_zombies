IC Zombies Terminal

The BBS version of IC Zombies, played here on an emulated 80×24 ANSI screen. It runs the real BBS door and the same rules as the web version, and online games are shared with both: join a game from the website or the BBS with its 4-letter code. Your saved game stays in this browser. On a phone, tap abc (or the screen) to type with your phone's keyboard.

IC Zombies · 80×24 · ANSI · CP437same game as the bbs

PlayingThe keys for each step are shown at the bottom of the screen. ? shows every key and what the map symbols mean.
OnlineOn the new-game screen, o then h hosts a game (you get a 4-letter code), or j joins one. Web and BBS players can join the same game.
QuittingPress q twice. Your game picks up at the start of the turn you were on next time you open this page in this browser.
